How to use

  1. Click the number buttons or type numbers on your keyboard.
  2. Choose an operation: + (add), − (subtract), × (multiply) or ÷ (divide).
  3. Press = or Enter to see the result.
  4. Use ⌫ or Backspace to delete one digit, and C or Esc to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this online calculator free?

Yes. The calculator is completely free, with no sign-up and no ads.

Can I use my keyboard?

Yes. Type numbers and + - * /, press Enter for equals, Backspace to delete and Escape to clear.

Does it follow the order of operations?

Yes. Multiplication and division are done before addition and subtraction, so 2+3×4 = 14.

What happens if I divide by zero?

The display shows Error. Press C or start typing a new number to continue.
